Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about phone voice agents.

How fast is the speech response time (latency)? ↓

VoxAgent operates at under 600ms total round-trip latency. By streaming WebSocket audio directly between Speech-to-Text, LLMs, and Text-to-Speech models, the AI responds as quickly as a human caller.

Can I port my existing business phone number? ↓

Yes! You can port your existing number directly via Twilio or simply configure conditional call forwarding on your current landline or mobile provider to route unanswered calls to VoxAgent.

What happens if the caller asks to speak to a human? ↓

The AI agent triggers a live call transfer function (`transfer_to_human`). It bridges the call to your specified office phone number while sending an instant transcript preview to your staff dashboard.
