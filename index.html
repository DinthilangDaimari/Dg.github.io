Place of the Means of Communication in Social Life

From Mobile Phones to Satellite TV

Introduction Mobile Phones Satellite TV Research Methods Conclusion

1. Introduction

Communication is an essential part of social life. It allows people to exchange information, express ideas, maintain relationships and share experiences. Communication has developed from letters and face-to-face conversations to mobile phones, television and digital networks.

2. Objectives of the Study

  • To understand the importance of communication.
  • To study the role of mobile phones in society.
  • To examine the influence of satellite television.
  • To understand effects on education, family and culture.
  • To identify benefits and challenges of modern media.

3. Mobile Phones and Social Life

Mobile phones allow people to call, message and communicate over long distances. They support education, business, emergency communication and access to information.

However, misinformation, online scams, privacy risks and excessive screen use are important concerns. Responsible use can help reduce these problems.

4. Satellite Television and Social Life

Satellite television uses communication satellites to distribute television programmes over wide areas. It provides news, educational programmes, sports, entertainment and cultural content.

Regional programmes can help audiences learn about local events, languages and traditions, including Assamese culture.

5. Other Means of Communication

  • Newspapers provide written news and information.
  • Radio broadcasts news, music and public announcements.
  • The internet supports research and digital communication.
  • Social media connects people and communities.

6. Research Methodology

This project may use observation, surveys, interviews and archival research. A survey can examine how people use mobile phones and television in daily life.

Any survey results and interview findings should be based on actual research rather than invented data.

7. Conclusion

Communication has an important place in modern social life. Mobile phones support personal communication, while satellite television provides information and entertainment to large audiences. Both can contribute to social development when used responsibly.
